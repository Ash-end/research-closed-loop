# Map literature gaps and method distinctions

Anchor search in the actual problem and candidate mechanism. A title, abstract, or retrieved paper does not establish its method, limits, or support for a particular claim.

## Procedure

1. Build queries around task/phenomenon, mechanism, constraints, neighboring-field terminology, and contrary results. Respect specified scope. Usually prioritize roughly two recent years while retaining foundational work, closest prior methods, strong baselines, and failure analyses—not prestigious venues alone.
2. Use available search tools to find primary papers and official supplements. Record date, channels, queries, coverage, and access limitations. Without search, provide a pending plan; do not fabricate papers, DOIs, or “latest SOTA.” Secondary summaries are leads.
3. From accessible text, record method operations, assumptions, data/protocols, performance/cost, and explicitly acknowledged limits. Separate author conclusions from your inferences. Locate support in sections, figures, or experimental settings and explain its relation to the claim.
4. Assess whether closest methods solve the problem, partly solve it, impose costs, or leave evidence unavailable. Not found is not nonexistent; an unevaluated dimension is not demonstrated failure.
5. Formulate a bounded gap: under X, limitation/cost Y leaves evidence insufficient for need Z; proposed operation M changes a mechanism and differs substantively from closest method N by D, with cost/counterexample R.

## Deliverable

Adapt the [claim–literature matrix](assets/gap-evidence.md). Provide key prior work, support locations, gap and uncertainty, and a testable design distinction—not a paper-count or idea-count exercise. If an existing method solves the practical problem, prefer reproducing or using it over forced novelty. Do not guarantee novelty or acceptance.

This is research, not competition scoring. Public read-only search does not authorize paid downloads, private-data uploads, or experiments. Respect source licenses; do not wholesale copy third-party skill implementations with restrictive licensing.
